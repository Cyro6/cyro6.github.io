# June 2025

**General Notes from Trip**

  * Beau and I went on this trip and I knew there was rain recently in the forecast. Barometric pressure was falling during this trip, it was cooler than normal due to the rain and overcast skies.

**Camping**

  * [Tourist Park Mondovi](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QuJRaeA8kzX9WizGA)

**Trip Cost** - $120

  * Gas - $0 - Used a gas card
  * Grocery - $60
  * Campsite - $60

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Scattered showers, mostly just overcast
  * Spot 1 - [Plum Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1puqHbaE4Zd3GNNJ9){:target="_blank"}
    * Absolute fantastic structure in this part of the stream. Saw some larger fish and only caught a few.
    * This is a really long section that I would love to do someday.
  * Spot 2 - [Little Bear Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BSZku2DXrwd3Htjp6){:target="_blank"}
    * Caught 1 keeper brookie.
    * Muddy water and mosquitos, not much else.
  * Spot 3 - [Bear Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gLsDmnkc6usaZG2z9){:target="_blank"}
    * More clear than the other spots. Further up stream.
    * 1 nice brown, couple smaller trout, less than 10 to hand.
  * Overall tough day, I think stream selection was one of the main problems here. If I had started at spot 3 I think things would have gone better.

**Day 2**

  * Weather - Scattered showers and cloudy, mostly just cloudy, around a high of 70
  * Spot 1 - [Elk Creek Big](https://maps.app.goo.gl/rWsdUduM5TDBrPhw8){:target="_blank"}
    * Caught probably 50 fish or so, absolute banger.

**Day 3**

  * Weather - Thunderstorm early in the morning 5-7 AM
  * Spot 1 - [Harvey Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VoPZiP3Z7eTudX8X9){:target="_blank"}
    * Caught probably 15 fish, however, they were all large Brook Trout. All between 12 and 14". Got my PB at 14.25". Absolutely wild.
