# May 2025

**General Notes from Trip**

  * This trip will arguably be one of the best fishing trips I will have ever had in a 4 day period. We caught a total of roughly 150 fish.

**Camping**

  * Tourist Park in Mondovi WI

**Trip Cost** - $202

  * Gas - $82
  * Grocery - $60
  * Campsite - $60 - Ben paid this in full.

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Sunny, clear skies, high of 75
  * Scouting - NA
  * Spot 1 - [Harvey Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/STW9GLCk1btqFJ939){:target="_blank"}
    * Got in at like 5:30-6 for an hour of fishing. Caught 2 really nice ones and a total of 7.

**Day 2**

  * Weather - Clear and sunny, hot. Probably 80 degrees.
  * Spot 1 - [Elk Creek Big](https://maps.app.goo.gl/p3WWnQzbp7Q55bg7A){:target="_blank"}
    * We only made it maybe ¼ of the way through this section. We forgot to bring food with us and Ben wanted lunch.
    * We caught a lot of fish in this section. Even a nice 17" Brown.
  * Spot 2 - [Elk Creek Big](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jgske49bkkamcPkV9){:target="_blank"}
    * We did this full section here, and caught a lot more fish.
  * This day was probably one of the best days fishing I have ever had. We caught probably 80+ fish. I am now looking for property to buy in the area.

**Day 3**

  * Weather - Clear and sunny, hot. Probably 80 degrees.
  * Spot 1 - [Waumandee Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dwiz7cbaSUcVaqWs9){:target="_blank"}
    * 2 nice brook trout and not much else to note. Maybe 8 fish total.
  * Spot 2 - [Little Waumandee Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dwFZJQfhWBNjXSCS6){:target="_blank"}
    * I am not sure we even caught a fish at this spot.
  * Spot 3 - [Elk Creek Big](https://maps.app.goo.gl/mkjTdcxWUzt1Fo5Y9){:target="_blank"}
    * Pivot to Elk Creek, we caught roughly 20-30 trout. I will circle back to update when I have firm numbers.

**Day 4**

  * Weather - Clear and sunny, hot. Probably 80 degrees. Windy
  * Spot 1 - [Elk Creek Big](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hRPgtUsfmbuVLzZY8){:target="_blank"}
    * Caught over 30 trout, this was a great day fishing. We started at the spot we ended at on Friday evening.
