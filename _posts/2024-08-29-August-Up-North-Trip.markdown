# August 2024

**General Notes from Trip**

  * We really struggled to find trout, we suspect we went up a bit early for good water temps, trout are still in their summer hiding phase and were not spread out and easy to find.

**Camping**

  * Up north property

**Trip Cost** - $25

  * Gas - $0
  * Grocery - $25
  * Campsite - $0

**Day 1**
  * Weather - Clear and sunny, very comfortable
  * Scouting - Popple and Pine off of 139
    * The Popple looked ok. There is a section lower than 139 that looks pretty flat and uninteresting from a structure perspective.
  * Spot 1 - Pine off of 139
    * The structure was fantastic, only caught chubs. We suspect water temp was an issue.
  * Spot 2 - Brule River North point off of 70
    * Structure and flowage was literally as perfect as you could ask for. No trout, didn't even see one. Water temp was likely still an issue and we should have gone further upstream to find trout. Although it may not have been enough.
  * Spot 3 - Otter Creek first put in off ATV trail
    * Super shallow, really easy wade, if water temp was lower this would have been spot on.
    * We hit up a couple further spots but it was great. Still too warm and all chubs.
    * Once you go far enough up it really is just a creek.

**Day 2**
  * Weather - Clear and sunny, very comfortable
  * Spot 1 - North Pike off of Lilly Lake Rd
    * Absolutely fantastic stream, no trout in this section. Still too low and/or middle of the stream.
  * Spot 2 - South Branch Pike River Horseshoe Falls
    * Deep above the falls, water temp is still too high.
    * We did manage to catch one trout here below the falls, sweeping the floor strategy.
  * Spot 3 - Further upstream on North Pike River
    * Managed to get on some actual Brook trout here. Managed about 10 or so but they were all small. We did see a really nice brown that was probably about 17".

**Day 3**
  * Weather - Clear and sunny, very comfortable
  * Spot 1 - Even further up on North Pike River, off of Pike River Drive
