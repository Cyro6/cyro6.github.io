# October 2025

**General Notes from Trip**

  * This trip was supposed to be a two day single overnight trip to lead into opening day. Sadly it rained so much leading up to the day I left and the during the day I was out there that I had to call it and head home. I still caught fish but everything was flooded out for counties around me that evening and my campsite was a swam.

**Camping**

  * [Kickapoo Reservation Campsite T](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2KXjRzk6b48Xocn19){:target="_blank"}

**Trip Cost** - $81

  * Gas - $60
  * Grocery - $0
  * Campsite - $21

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Cloudy, Rain, Thunderstorm, Cold
  * Spot 1 - [Coon Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7YERGGycthRZwdsd6){:target="_blank"}
    * 1 trout in this spot between here and the bridge. Pretty sure I snagged, wasn't even a foul-hook.
  * Spot 2 - [Timber Coulee Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/xv4n3N75f3p8CEC57){:target="_blank"}
    * 15 trout over the span of a few hours. Rained almost the entire time I fished. The rain seemed to ease the trout into a false sense of safety, way more willing to come out of hiding.
