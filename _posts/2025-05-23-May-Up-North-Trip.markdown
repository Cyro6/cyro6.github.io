# May 2025

**General Notes from Trip**

  * Given the past challenges with fishing up north I tried a few different strategies to get on fish for this trip.

**Camping**

  * Up north property

**Trip Cost** - $60

  * Gas - $60
  * Grocery - $0
  * Campsite - $0

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Clear
  * Scouting - NA
  * Spot 1 - [Peshtigo River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/39x2qigs2trkKiZF8){:target="_blank"}
    * At the dam, I had a lot of followers and connected with a few but did not land any, pretty frustrating.
  * Spot 2 - [Otter Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NyCKoXFUAftngw5m6){:target="_blank"}
    * Difficult to fish but I did land about 7 Brooks, two nice keepers 10.5 and 11.5.

**Day 2**

  * Weather - Partly cloudy with scattered showers
  * Spot 1 - [At Camp](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pZrsZorRYzgiuxpn9){:target="_blank"}
    * Fished around camp and managed another keeper at "The Point". Later in the evening I tried canoeing with Britney up the river a bit, she would anchor to the shore and I would cast, managed another nice fish doing this. I felt like this was a good strategy to cover ground quickly and to hit good spots rather than wading the entire way.

**Day 3**

  * Weather - Clear and sunny, very comfortable
  * Spot 1 - [At Camp](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pZrsZorRYzgiuxpn9){:target="_blank"}
    * Beau and I took the canoe upstream for a good 3 hours or so. The water level was pretty much perfect in order to walk through some of the harder sections. I caught maybe 8 Brook Trout over 3-4 hours of fishing, it was a lot of work for only a few but it was fun.

**Day 4**

  * Weather - Clear and sunny
  * Spot 1 - [At Camp](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pZrsZorRYzgiuxpn9){:target="_blank"}
    * Jack and I took the canoe downstream, there are some pretty serious rapids further down that we avoided. With Jack I didn't want to risk having any problems so we didn't even approach the rapids. There was a spot where it narrowed with some rocks where I managed a nice Brook trout and missed out on like 4-5 other chances.
