**General Notes from Trip**  
  * This trip was a consistent one, probably 2-3 trout per hour with a total fishing time of 16 or so hours, a lot of work. 
 
**Camping**   
  * [Eau Galle Highridge Campground](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kZzkvRk3bms7DTKb7)

**Trip Cost**
  * Gas - $60  
  * Grocery - $80  
  * Campsite - $72

**Day 1**  
  * Weather - Cloudy  
  * Spot 1 - [Plum Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XgxRyHYWscmpv8ZH8)  
    * I just love these sections of the stream that are untouched by HI.  
    * Not a lot of fish but I caught several 12”+ Browns with a nice 15” brown, had a really close call with a 18+ but didn’t connect.  

**Day 2**  
  * Weather - Cloudy, a bit of rain the night before.  
  * Spot 1  - [Gilbert Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DNwWCCjxXjsioTnH7)  
    * Another long section here, it started off with me missing two 18+ inch browns, I would say this stream is a tough fish but the chances of getting a biggen are pretty decent.  
    * 10 or so to hand.  
  * Spot 2 - [Isabelle Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GwCt6EHeureDSz1BA)  
    * Somewhat short section here, I really had fun fishing this section, it was very unique. 15-20 to hand. 

**Day 3**  
  * Weather - Mostly Cloudy  
  * Spot 1 - [Elk Creek "Big"](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nVnFQbbzHBp6RxQo8)  
    * A short section of Elk Creek here, we found a good amount of Brookies but it was not holding anything bigger than 12 inches, I never even saw a big boy. 20 or so to hand.
