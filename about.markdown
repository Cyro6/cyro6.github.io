## The Log

Before I was born, my grandfather kept a log of his fishing trips. Not digital, not a blog — a scrapbook, filled with notes, dates, and memories from his time on the water. He died before I ever got the chance to fish with him, but somehow the love for trout fishing found its way into my soul anyway. In 2024 I decided to start doing what he did — logging my trips, keeping track of the fish, the streams, the conditions, and the memories. This site is that log, just in a more modern form.

---

## The Journey

My name is Chase DeVrou and I've been chasing stream trout in Wisconsin since 2019. My first outing was a single day on a small trout stream in Sheboygan County. I came home disappointed and didn't try again for nearly a year. When I did go back, I managed three trout — but the fishing in Sheboygan County is limited, the streams are few, and I knew there had to be something better out there.

I shifted my focus to the **Central Sands** region of Wisconsin — so named for the sandy, glacially-deposited soils that make up the central part of the state, creating cold, spring-fed streams that are ideal trout habitat. I started catching more fish, mixing Central Sands trips with excursions up north, covering a lot of ground across the state. On a really good day or weekend I might get 20 fish to hand, but I kept seeing people on YouTube catching far more. I knew I hadn't found my spot yet.

---

## The Driftless

In April of 2022, my brother and I made our first trip to the **Driftless Area** — the unglaciated region of southwest Wisconsin characterized by its rugged bluffs, cold spring-fed streams, and some of the finest wild trout fishing in the Midwest. The fishing was absolutely fantastic. That trip changed everything.

I made three more trips to the Driftless later that year, and by 2023 it had become my primary destination. The rolling hills, the clear streams, the wild brown trout — it was exactly what I had been looking for. I still fish the Central Sands occasionally since it's closer to home, and I make a trip or two each year to my parents' property in Northern Wisconsin, but my heart is in the Driftless.

## How I Fish

I am strictly a spinner fisherman — but not with store-bought gear. I build my own spinners by hand, combining **tungsten, brass, and nickel hardware**. I have been experiementing with **3D printed components** that I design myself for color and profile. 

I usually fish with my brother, my sons, my brother-in-laws, or my dad. Trout fishing has become a family affair, and some of my best days on the water have been shared with them.
