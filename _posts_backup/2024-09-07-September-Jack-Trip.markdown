# September 2024

**General Notes from Trip**

  * We really struggled to find trout, I suspect we went up a bit early for good water temps, trout are still in their summer hiding phase and were not spread out and easy to find. We started out too low on the stream, a lesson I should have learned from my previous trip out.

**Camping**

  * [Wildcat Mountain State Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HnwvdZ7cF6qRvrE37){:target="_blank"}

**Trip Cost** - $114

  * Gas - $60
  * Grocery - $30
  * Campsite - $24

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Cooler than the rest of the week but clear skies.
  * Spot 1 - [Little LaCrosse River near J](https://maps.app.goo.gl/n56jDMdDZCq97YSJ7){:target="_blank"}
    * This was a spot Ben and I crushed in April. I thought for sure we would have another smackdown. We didn't, I saw one trout and called it to go to a different spot on the stream.
  * Spot 2 - [Little LaCrosse River - further upstream](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Xb34AdLxwpz8y8L4A){:target="_blank"}
    * Further up stream by a few miles working thick stuff. Managed 3 fish here, 2 keepers, one of those was a 16" Brown.
    * I personally really like this section but it's work to fish.
  * Spot 3 - [Little LaCrosse River - upper access](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CKdgStbESj54Q7Mh8){:target="_blank"}
    * Further up checking out an access point.
    * This spot has some deep pools but is pretty open to the elements, lots of fish and lots of potential.
