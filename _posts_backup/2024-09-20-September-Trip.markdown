# September 2024

**General Notes from Trip**

  * It rained the night before we started fishing, this completely threw off my morning plans and I wasted time going to streams that were muddied and high. I could have fished these with Ben but with Jack I had to look for lower and clearer streams to make wading easier. We did find a few eventually and we found fish but sadly things didn't go perfectly.

**Camping**

  * Mondovi Tourist Park

**Trip Cost** - $130

  * Gas - $60
  * Grocery - $30
  * Campsite - $40

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Rained heavily the evening before, I should have checked recent precipitation reports to help identify a stream that would be more fishable.
  * Spot 1 - [Elk Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GRf8RfB1ds24RiS86){:target="_blank"}
    * High and muddy, the stream looked fantastic otherwise, I would love to fish this stream a different time.
  * Spot 2 - [Harvey Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/14zdnQ9tg52WsWYz7){:target="_blank"}
    * High and muddy.
  * Spot 3 - [Eau Galle River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yEF6PkCHqFLk2JGy5){:target="_blank"}
    * Clear and perfectly leveled, I really liked this section of stream.
    * Caught a few browns all under 10 inches, probably 3 total.
  * Spot 4 - [Plum Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pRemJsRHxubuzbnY9){:target="_blank"}
    * Good water levels, I loved this section of the stream, it looks like it gets really low traffic.
    * Caught probably 10-15 trout here, only one was a keeper at exactly 12 inches.
  * Spot 5 - [Harvey Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/upsHVa2kpQkLqxU29){:target="_blank"}
    * Literally only fished by the bridge and caught a monster 14 inch Brook Trout.

**Day 2**

  * Weather - Storms heading our way
  * Spot 1 - [Harvey Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7stFtfcRGE4N2Btb9){:target="_blank"}
    * Didn't even see a fish, decided to head up stream.
  * Spot 2 - [Harvey Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KvKmqEHVE68YTc6L9){:target="_blank"}
    * Caught 3 Brook trout all 9-10 inches.
