# April 2024

**General Notes from Trip**

  * Pre-season opener trip with a drive in Friday evening and fishing Saturday through Monday.

**Camping**

  * [Wildcat Mountain State Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/iMiQWguBLxmvQPxf6){:target="_blank"}

**Trip Cost** - $152.92

  * Gas - $60
  * Grocery - $40
  * Campsite - $52.95

**Day 1**
  * Left Friday night, had extra room in the car, even with the rain cover Dad let us borrow.
  * Rained overnight a bit, expecting a Thunderstorm on Saturday.

**Day 2**
  * Weather - Turned out to be an absolute gem of a day with 50/50 clouds and sun. The evening had a nice thunderstorm roll in. 60-75 degrees all day.
  * Spot 1 - [West Fork Kickapoo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9wfRDm11EKa2vreR6){:target="_blank"}
    * This ended up not really working out, we found one spot but only landed a small brown and a chub.
  * Spot 2 - [Little Lacrosse River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6iy2cveok9ganiSu6){:target="_blank"}
    * Great fishing, probably 30+ to hand between the both of us. Pretty straight forward wade with mix between timber, cuts, shelves and pools.
    * I really liked this location for fishing. It was a somewhat unknown put in and the stretch was fantastic, we got out at the little creek that feeds in just before the private bridge.

**Day 3**
  * Weather - Rained all day with it being a bit heavier in the afternoon. Around 60.
  * Spot 1 - [Billings Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jDJHeocYjaBqVeqy8){:target="_blank"}
    * Beautiful stream with awesome cliff edges, I suspect this gets fished a good amount but on this day was not getting fished due to the weather.
    * Caught 10+ to hand in the morning with consistent rainfall, water was clear.
  * Spot 2 - [Billings Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WECyagYJExv4Chw19){:target="_blank"}
    * Repeat of the morning with more rain, eventually the water started to mud up and probably cool down from the added rain, fish started to shutdown into the evening.

**Day 4**
  * Weather - Windy and cool, probably a solid low pressure system. No rain but chilled. Probably around 60, the wind had a bite.
  * Spot 1 - [Little Lacrosse River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7RdNb84Ju8ad8puu5){:target="_blank"}
    * One spot up from the Saturday spot, probably should have gone further up considering the rain on Sunday. I liked this spot and I suspect it really gets good once you get into the woods but we ran out of time for that section.
