# April 2025

**General Notes from Trip**

  * This trip was filled with success, we caught around 70 fish in the 4 days we fished. The weather was both cold and hot. The last day was the most challenging.

**Camping**

  * [Eau Galle High Ridge Campground](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/233510){:target="_blank"}

**Trip Cost** - $226

  * Gas - $82
  * Grocery - $60 (probably more than this but this is a rough estimate)
  * Campsite - $84

**Day 1**

  * Weather - Cool, rainy in the morning a bit, overcast
  * Scouting - NA
  * Spot 1 - [Plum Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QMK8pQtX58F1ho4j7){:target="_blank"}
    * I really liked this location, this section is just awesome, constant timber and holes.
    * Only got roughly 10 to hand.

**Day 2**

  * Weather - Morning drizzle, cool and cloudy, brief moments of sun
  * Spot 1 - [Eau Galle River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/x3p9kofDpfnVD5pJ6){:target="_blank"}
    * Tough fishing, the water was muddy and moving fast, got 3 fish or so.
  * Spot 2 - [Eau Galle River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/cTkPyGH8g3RbXh4i7){:target="_blank"}
    * Way over corrected on heading up stream to get out of the muddy stuff.
    * 1 to hand.
  * Spot 3 - [Gilbert Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ehWpXPaBqcGWCuSaA){:target="_blank"}
    * Very clear water, 1 to hand. Too far up stream.
  * Spot 4 - [Gilbert Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JWgw6Bp4ZZSFokgg9){:target="_blank"}
    * Over corrected and went too far downstream.
    * Caught 2 but it was pretty muddy water down this low.
    * Really cool Eagles nest.
  * Spot 5 - [Gilbert Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6hMpCy8hvMLga8aF9){:target="_blank"}
    * Struck a happy middle point in the stream, connected with more fish. 6 more to hand or so. It was less muddy.
  * Overall this was a challenging day and we caught around 10 fish. I think we should have tried to give Eau Galle another try somewhere in the middle of the stream rather than dipping out to Gilbert after the upper section was so clear.

**Day 3**

  * Weather - Clear and sunny, very comfortable
  * Spot 1 - [Isabelle Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VrMKA3TLTEKucP7v9){:target="_blank"}
    * Saw a lot of fish, they were not interested in connecting with the spinners.
  * Spot 2 - [Trimbelle River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bAYEnNWfXGZTBbcC6){:target="_blank"}
    * Absolute banger, we caught 30 fish or so before getting out at the road and eating lunch.
  * Spot 3 - [Trimbelle River](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZRbYWN9TRSxQptxP8){:target="_blank"}
    * Fish started shutting down compared to earlier in the day, connected with about 10 more fish.
  * Everything came together on this day, we caught about 40 fish. The biggest was a 14" Brown.

**Day 4**

  * Weather - Drizzle/rain in the morning, cleared later in the day
  * Spot 1 - [Bear Creek](https://maps.app.goo.gl/T3N81XK1jFokAvKD8){:target="_blank"}
    * Muddy, had a few swipes but we called it due to how muddy it was.
    * We should have moved to a different stream at this point given our time limitation but pressed on.
  * Spot 2 - [Bear Creek Trib Access](https://maps.app.goo.gl/obCeKbUxur5GanbaA){:target="_blank"}
    * If the fish are in Timber this spot could be insane, BUT they were in pools, thus this spot was a slog and only resulted in 4 fish, 3 of which came out of the same hole. I could see late season, hitting this spot up but I won't be revisiting this one.
  * Tough day, this felt similar to day 2, we made the wrong call by going too far up stream in order to find clear water, I think we would have been better off from a time management perspective if we had just stuck with either the middle spot, sticking it out when it was muddy and waiting for the sun or moving on to a different stream. In a way this was a scouting mission and that was a success.
